# Changelog

What changed in each release. A release's section is its notes: on GitHub, and in Livepaper's update window, through the appcast. A release refuses to start without one.

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- Live wallpapers on the desktop and the lock screen, from a video or a Wallpaper Engine scene.
- Wallpaper Engine's Workshop, with your own Steam login.
- A wallpaper per display, playlists that rotate, and pausing while the desktop is covered, on battery or in Low Power Mode.
- Updates through Sparkle, checked once a day and installed when you click for them.
- If Livepaper is opened from the disk image, it asks you to drag it to the Applications folder first.
